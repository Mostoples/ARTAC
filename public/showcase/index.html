Smart TB medication dispenser

No dose
goes unseen.

ARTA-C presses each caplet out of the original blister, checks that the right patient is taking it, and tells the treatment supervisor (PMO) the moment it happens.

28caplets per blister
3servos, one plunger
180days, every day
01 · Load

Like bread
into a toaster.

The PMO clips the whole blister into a holder and drops it into the top slot. The medicine never leaves its foil until the moment it is taken.

Original blisterRack & gearRow by row
02 · Dispense

A plunger that
pecks, not pours.

The holder lowers one row at a time. A carriage slides to the right column and a scotch-yoke plunger presses the dome until the caplet breaks through the foil and rolls into the tray.

≥28 N push4 columns × 7 rowsExact dose
03 · Verify

The right person,
at the right time.

ESP32-CAM confirms the registered patient's face. A light-sealed finger tunnel with a MAX30102 PPG sensor reads heart rate and SpO₂, and the dose is logged to the ARTA-C cloud for the PMO and doctor.

Face checkHeart rate · SpO₂Live to PMO

Blood pressure and glucose from PPG are experimental estimates, not a diagnosis.

04 · Inside

29 printed parts.
One quiet machine.

Fully 3D-printed in PLA and PETG, 168 × 206 × 54 mm, rechargeable over USB-C, with a 2000 mAh battery for travel and power cuts.

3× MG90SESP32 + LVGLLiPo + USB-C
